# Contributing

DRAX is currently a documentation and research repository. Protocol, smart-contract, wallet or deployment code is out of scope.

Useful contributions include:

- corrections supported by primary legal, economic or protocol sources;
- translation corrections that preserve the normative terminology;
- accessibility improvements and accurate image descriptions;
- threat-model objections, counterexamples and falsifiable failure scenarios;
- corrections to broken sources, dates or public statistics.

Every contribution must preserve the public status notice: independent feasibility study, no government mandate, no legal tender, no live asset and no investment offer.

Please open an issue before proposing a structural rewrite. Do not submit token contracts, sale mechanics, wallet binaries, exchange listings, private documents or promotional price claims.
