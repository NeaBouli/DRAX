# Source and verification register

Reviewed 1 September 2026. For legal and technical claims, rely on the linked primary materials rather than this website’s summary.

1. [Kaspa Docs — Toccata](https://docs.kaspa.org/toccata) and [Agent Brief](https://docs.kaspa.org/toccata/agent-brief): UTXO-native covenants, Transaction V1 and development model.
2. [Silverscript](https://github.com/kaspanet/silverscript): language and compiler status; reassess maturity before implementation.
3. [vProgs](https://github.com/kaspanet/vprogs): based-application reference; treat as an evolving stack.
4. [Article 128 TFEU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/treaty/tfeu_2012/art_128/oj): euro banknotes and legal-tender status.
5. [Regulation 974/98](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/1998/974/oj): introduction of the euro.
6. [MiCA — Regulation (EU) 2023/1114](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1114/oj).
7. [Bank of Greece — MiCA](https://www.bankofgreece.gr/en/main-tasks/supervision/regulation-eu-2023_1114-on-markets-in-crypto-assets): Law 5193/2025 and competent authorities.
8. [Transfer Rule — Regulation (EU) 2023/1113](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1113/oj).
9. [AMLR — Regulation (EU) 2024/1624](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1624/oj).
10. [ECB — Digital euro FAQ](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/digital_euro/faqs/html/ecb.faq_digital_euro.en.html).
11. [UNCTAD — Review of Maritime Transport 2025](https://unctad.org/system/files/official-document/rmt2025overview_en.pdf).
12. [Bank of Greece — Travel services 2025](https://www.bankofgreece.gr/en/news-and-media/press-office/news-list/news?announcement=27a32287-1c58-44e7-b900-2c92c53ffe9c).
13. [BIS Annual Economic Report 2025](https://www.bis.org/publications/aer-2025/next-generation-monetary-financial-system).
14. [IMF — Currency Boards and Lender of Last Resort](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/007/1999/009/article-A004-en.xml).

1kUSD is mentioned solely as methodological experience for invariants, threat modelling, known limitations, telemetry and sunset discipline. Internal files, the USD peg, PSM, oracle design and EVM architecture are not a source or foundation for DRX.
